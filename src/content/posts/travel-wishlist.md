## Wishlist

### 1. Landour, Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

A quiet hill station above Mussoorie. Famous for the Char Dukan market, Lal Tibba viewpoint, and being the long-time home of Ruskin Bond.

---

### 2. Narkanda, Himachal Pradesh

A small town on the old Hindustan-Tibet road in the Shimla district, known for apple orchards and Hatu Peak. Relatively untouched.

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yLqWJS1RnKeoCam48)

---

### 3. Anini, Arunachal Pradesh

The headquarters of Dibang Valley district — one of the most remote and least visited places in India. Near the McMahon Line. Requires an Inner Line Permit.

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pcS1ioGvEHsQsh39A)
